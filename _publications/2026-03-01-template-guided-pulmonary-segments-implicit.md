High-quality 3D models of pulmonary segments support segmentectomy and thoracic surgical planning. This work learns neural implicit surfaces by deforming a template toward patient-specific anatomy, combining efficiency of sparse/implicit representations with topology-aware segment reconstruction. We introduce complementary evaluation metrics aligned with clinical use and release **Lung3D**, a dataset of hundreds of labeled pulmonary segments with vasculature context. Code and resources are available from the authors’ project page ([ImPulSe](https://github.com/HINTLab/ImPulSe)).
